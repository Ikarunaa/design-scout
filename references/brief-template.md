Save as `design-brief.md` in the project. Fill every section with a specific choice. Delete the guidance lines in italics.

## Checkpoint summary

What to show the user for approval is a cut-down version of the above, readable in a minute:

- Concept line
- 3 or 4 strongest references, as links, with one line each on what is being borrowed
- Palette (hex) and fonts
- Section outline, one line per section
- Signature element
- Direction B in two lines, if the research split
- Assumptions to confirm
